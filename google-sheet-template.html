📈 Budget Tracking (Analysis)
What this is: Compare your actual sales to your daily budget target. See trends and performance.
Date Daily Budget Actual Sales Variance % of Target Status
08/05/2026 $3,200.00 $2,450.50 -$749.50 76.6% ⚠ Under Budget
08/04/2026 $3,200.00 $2,750.00 -$450.00 85.9% ⚠ Under Budget
08/03/2026 $3,200.00 $2,100.00 -$1,100.00 65.6% ⚠ Under Budget
Formulas to use:
Column D (Variance): =C2-B2
Column E (% of Target): =C2/B2*100
Column F (Status): =IF(C2>=B2, "✓ On Track", "⚠ Under Budget")
How to set your daily budget:
Example: If your monthly goal is $80,000 ÷ 25 business days = $3,200/day
Adjust based on busy vs. slow days. Update the "Daily Budget" column with your target.
