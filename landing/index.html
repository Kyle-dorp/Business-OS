02The arithmetic

Disconnected systems don't just cost subscriptions.

The subscriptions are the part you can see on a statement. The expensive part is what leaks between them — counts that don't match, hours nobody reconciled, a number that was right in one place and wrong in another.

1.6% of sales lost to shrinkage from spoilage, theft and miscounts when inventory lives apart from sales — around $16,000 a year at a $1M location. Point B, 2025
5–10 hrs a week of staff time spent reconciling systems by hand that were never built to talk to each other. Crunchtime
74% of operators say technology integration directly affects their profitability — not their convenience, their margin. Nat'l Restaurant Assoc.
$5k–50k to integrate the tools you already pay for. Connecting the stack routinely costs more than replacing it. Point B, 2025
